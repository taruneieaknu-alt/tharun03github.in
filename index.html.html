Active Cell Balancing of Lithium-ion Batteries for an EV

A 900-cell (30S×30P) battery pack, modeled to behave like a real EV pack and built with MATLAB's Battery Builder app. The goal: correct the SOC imbalance that forms between cells due to manufacturing and aging differences — without wasting energy as heat the way passive balancing does.
